Direction B / Editorial

Same content as Direction A. A louder, more typographic argument.

Where Direction A whispers, this one states. Pure black on pure white, a single marker yellow used on exactly two elements page-wide, and headline type set large enough to be read across a room. Services, work and process are set as indexes rather than cards — the page reads like a body of work, not a brochure. Best if we want the rebrand to feel like a deliberate break from the current site.

04

From first call to first commit

Step 01

Scoping call

30 minutes on the problem, your stack and how your team works. You leave with a written recommendation on team shape, whether or not you hire us.

Step 02

Engineer shortlist

We put forward named engineers with the relevant production experience. You interview them yourself — we never place someone you haven’t met.

Step 03

Paid trial sprint

Two weeks on real work, in your repo, with defined success criteria. Walk away at the end with no further commitment.

Step 04

Scale or hand over

Grow the pod as the roadmap grows, or run a structured handover to your in-house team. Both are planned from the start.
